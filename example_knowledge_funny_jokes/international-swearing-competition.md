# International Swearing Competition

An international swearing competition. Three finalists: an American, a Russian, and a Pole.

The finalists were placed in tightly sealed booths so they couldn't hear or see each other. The judges gave the signal. The American went first:

> — Fuck, motherfucker, fuck yourself... etc., etc. And so on for about five minutes.

Then the Russian:

> — Your mother's cunt, bitch, fuck you in the ass... etc., etc.

After ten minutes of intense swearing, it was the Pole's turn. There was a slight commotion in his booth, and then he launched into a tirade:

> — Oh, you fucking son of a bitch, goat-assed motherfucker, fuck you and your mother too... etc., etc.

The Pole kept going for about an hour. Suddenly, he stuck his head out of the booth and said:

> — Okay, I can start now.

The judges replied:

> — Start?! What was that for the last hour?!

> — That? Nothing. My shoelace came undone, the booth is fucking cramped, and I couldn't tie the damn thing...
