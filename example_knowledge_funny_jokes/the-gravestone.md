# The Gravestone

A teacher asks her students during class:

> — If I died... what would you write on my gravestone?

Little Mary:

> — May God watch over her soul.

Little Kazik:

> — Peace to her soul.

Little Krystyna:

> — Rest in peace.

While the children were answering, Johnny was playing with a pen, taking it apart. Suddenly, the spring fell onto the floor. Johnny crawled under the desk and started looking for it.

The teacher said:

> — Wonderful, very good... Would anyone else like to say what they would write on my gravestone if I died?

And then it happened...

At that very moment, Johnny found the spring and shouted:

> — Oh, you fucking bitch, there you are!
