# Politics

Johnny comes to his father and asks:

> — Dad, what is politics?

> — Politics, son, is capitalism. I'm capitalism because I earn money. Your mother is the government because she controls the money. Grandpa is the trade union because he does nothing. The maid is the working class because she cleans and works. You, son, are the people because you make up society. And your little brother is the future.

> — Dad, I'll sleep on it and maybe I'll understand.

Johnny went to bed. During the night, he was woken by his baby brother crying because he had soiled his diaper.

Johnny went to his mother's room, but couldn't wake her, so he went to the maid's room.

He walked in and found the maid busy with his father, while Grandpa was peeking through the window. Nobody noticed Johnny, so he quickly left, went back to his room, comforted his little brother, and went to sleep.

In the morning, Johnny got up and went downstairs.

His father asked:

> — So, Johnny, do you know what politics is now?

> — Yes, Daddy, I do.

> — So, what is it?

> — Capitalism exploits the working class, the trade unions watch it happen, the government is asleep, the people are being ignored, and the future is lying in shit.

That's politics, Daddy.
