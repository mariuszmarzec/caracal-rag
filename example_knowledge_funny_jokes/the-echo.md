# The Echo

A Pole, a Russian, and a German are walking through a forest when suddenly the Devil stops them.

> — If you say something and the echo can be heard for 20 minutes, I'll let you go.

The German went first:

> — Heil Hitler!

The voice echoed for five minutes.

Next was the Russian:

> — Zdravstvuyte! *(Hello!)*

The voice echoed for ten minutes.

Finally, it was the Pole's turn:

> — They're giving away vodka!

*"Where?!"* echoed through the forest for two hours.
