# The Teddy Bear

Mom gave Johnny her last 50 złoty to buy groceries. There were still two weeks until payday.

She said:

> — Johnny, buy some bread, margarine, and a piece of cheese. Bring the rest of the money home.

Johnny went to the store, but on the way he saw a teddy bear he really liked. It cost exactly 50 złoty. He bought it and ran home.

His mother said:

> — Johnny, what have you done?! Go and sell that teddy bear immediately.

Johnny went without hesitation to sell the bear to a neighbor. He entered her apartment and found her in bed with some man.

Suddenly, someone knocked on the door.

The neighbor shoved the man and Johnny into a closet.

Johnny said to the man:

> — Buy the teddy bear.

> — Get lost, kid.

> — Or I'll start screaming.

> — Here's your fifty złoty. Now shut up.

> — Give me back the teddy bear.

> — I'm not giving it back.

> — Give it back or I'll scream.

The same situation happened several times. Johnny made a hell of a lot of money.

He returned home with groceries: caviar, shrimp, ham, and a huge pile of cash, which he put on the table.

His mother said:

> — Johnny, you must have robbed a bank. Go to the priest and confess immediately!

Johnny went to church, approached the confessional, and said:

> — I'm here about the teddy bear.

> — GOODBYE! I don't have any money left!
