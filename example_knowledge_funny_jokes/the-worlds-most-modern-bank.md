# The World's Most Modern Bank

The world's most modern bank, with an insanely secure vault.

The owners decided to test the security. They invited the world's most famous thieves from France, the United States, Germany, and two random Poles.

For each team, they would turn off the lights in front of the vault for half a minute. If, after the lights came back on, the vault was open, the team could take everything inside.

The French went first. The lights came back on — they hadn't managed it.

Then the Americans. The lights came back on — they hadn't even had time to take their equipment out.

Then the Germans. The lights came back on — nothing.

Finally, it was the Poles' turn.

The lights went out. Half a minute passed. They tried to turn the lights back on — nothing. They tried again — nothing.

From the darkness, only a quiet whisper could be heard:

> — Fuck, Stefan, we've got so much money. What the hell do we need this fucking light bulb for?
